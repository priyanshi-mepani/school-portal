How to Apply

Follow these simple steps to apply and enroll in our courses:

  1. Visit our Student Portal homepage and create an account.
  2. Login with your registered email and password.
  3. Browse the available courses on the Courses page.
  4. Select the course(s) you want to enroll in.
  5. Fill in all required details and submit your application.
  6. Check your email for a confirmation message and further instructions.
  7. Once approved, you can start accessing course materials and assignments.
